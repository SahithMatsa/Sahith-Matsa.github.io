Case Study: Business Process Re-Engineering

An end-to-end operations flow for an in-home sales and installation business, from the first sales appointment to installation, quality assurance, and final payment. I mapped how work moves across five teams and presented recommendations to improve the process flow.

Thompson Creek Co.Business Analyst and Strategy Intern, 2024Process mappingSAP order flowCross-team hand-offs

What the redesigned flow does

  • Puts every step under one owner, color-coded across Resource Planning, Customer Success, Procurement, Inventory Management, and In-House Construction.
  • Makes the decision points explicit: whether the sale closes, the Pre-Con result, and the installation QA result.
  • Adds payment gates: a down payment after contract approval, and final payment after installation paperwork and a successful QA.
  • Holds the delivery block on the sales order until materials are validated, so installation is only scheduled when materials are ready.
Resource PlanningCustomer SuccessProcurementInventory ManagementIn-House Construction

1. Sales appointment (IHPD)

Allocate sales resource›Dispatch IHPD to sales resource›Perform IHPD›Sales closed?›Schedule Pre-Con

If the sale is not closed, the result is recorded as IHPD (No) and the flow ends. If closed, it moves to pre-construction.

2. Pre-construction (Pre-Con)

Allocate PM resource›Prepare Pre-Con paperwork›Dispatch Pre-Con to PM›Meet customer, take measurements›Set installation date›Pre-Con result?›Contract approval after all changes›Process down payment

If the Pre-Con result is negative, the sale is marked not closed.

3. Procurement

Update quote in SAP›Convert quote to purchase orders›Create purchase orders›Submit orders to suppliers›Receive supplier acknowledgements›Validate material deliveries›Ready for installation: remove delivery block on sales order›Contact customer for installation

In parallel, Procurement creates the stock transfer order for Inventory and the labour purchase order for installation. A future installation date possibility is checked at purchase order creation.

4. Inventory management

Receive orders from suppliers›Perform checks and scans›Review stock transfer order›Transfer products to warehouse nearest the customer›Stage materials for installation›Create delivery in SAP›Equip installer team with tools, materials, information

5. Installation

Allocate installation resource›Prepare installation paperwork›Dispatch to installation resources›Visit home and perform installation›Complete installation›Receive all installation paperwork›Process final payment

6. Quality assurance and close-out

Allocate PM resource for QA›Prepare QA paperwork›Dispatch QA visit›Visit customer to perform QA›Installation result?›Obtain final payment

A successful QA result releases the final payment.
